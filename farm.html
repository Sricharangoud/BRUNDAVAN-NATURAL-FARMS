THE SACRED GIR BREED

The Glory of Indigenous Bos Indicus Cows

The native Gir cow of India is revered in ancient Ayurvedic treatises as *Kamadhenu* — the provider of health, vitality, and longevity.

Unlike foreign cross-bred cattle (Jersey / Holstein Friesian), indigenous Gir cows possess a prominent hump housing the sacred *Surya Ketu Nadi*. This special vein absorbs solar energy and synthesizes gold salts into their milk, lending authentic A2 milk its natural golden hue, rich carotene, and therapeutic digestibility.

Their milk naturally contains only 100% A2 Beta-Casein protein, free from the BCM-7 peptide found in industrial A1 milk that causes lactose distress and inflammatory gut issues.

ETHICAL FARM PROTOCOLS

How We Care For Our Cattle Daily

🍼

Calf-First Milking

The newborn calf drinks its mother's milk to full satiety from two teats first. Only the remaining milk is gently collected for human consumption.

🌿

Ayurvedic Fodder Diet

Cows graze on organic green fodder, Super Napier grass, jowar stalks, and seasonal therapeutic herbs like Shatavari, Ashwagandha, and mineral-rich rock salt.

🎶

Vedic Chantings & Music

Classical flute melodies, Krishna bhajans, and Vedic mantras are played softly in our open-air shelters, fostering peaceful, joyful hormones in the herd.

🕊️

Lifelong Sanctuary

We never abandon or sell non-milking, elderly, or unwell cows. Every cow is sheltered with tender medical care, nutritious meals, and love until the natural end of life.

👐

Hand-Milking in Brass

We strictly prohibit automated vacuum suction machines that bruise cow udders. Skilled village milkmen milk by hand using sanitized, sterile brass vessels.

💧

Sweet Aquifer Borewell Water

Our Gaushala draws crystal-clear, mineral-balanced sweet water from deep natural groundwater aquifers, providing unlimited pure hydration to the cattle.

A DAY AT BRUNDAVAN GAUSHALA

The Natural Sunrise-to-Sunset Rhythm

5:00 AM – 6:30 AM Morning Care & Milking

Gaushala washdown with clean water, gentle cow massage, calf feeding, and traditional brass hand-milking.

8:00 AM – 2:00 PM Open Pasture Roaming

The herd grazes freely across 25 acres of organic green pastures under warm sunlight, socializing naturally.

3:00 PM – 5:00 PM Ayurvedic Fodder & Rest

Rest in ventilated, shaded earthen sheds. Feeding of crushed grain, mineral salts, and fresh green Napier fodder.

5:30 PM – 7:00 PM Sunset Aarti & Care

Calves join their mothers for second feeding, evening milk collection, dhoop incense fumigation for mosquitoes, and aarti.
