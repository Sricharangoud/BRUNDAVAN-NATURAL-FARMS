About Our Business & Enterprise

Brundavan Natural Farms is a family-owned agro-ecological enterprise and sacred Gir cow sanctuary founded with a clear purpose: to revive traditional Vedic food wisdom, protect indigenous Indian cattle, and deliver medicinal-grade natural nutrition directly to households.

Headquartered near the historic Kakatiya Heritage Canal in Warangal, Telangana, our agribusiness operates on strict Ahimsa (non-violent) principles. Unlike commercial industrial dairies, our calves drink their fill first, our cows graze freely on organic pastures without tethering, and old cows are sheltered with lifelong reverence rather than sold to slaughterhouses.

By operating as a Direct-to-Consumer (D2C) farm collective, we eliminate commercial intermediaries and distributors. This ensures complete cold-chain purity for our customers while paying sustainable, dignified wages to rural milkmen, cowherds, and traditional wood-press oil artisans across the Telangana countryside.

ENTERPRISE VERTICALS

Core Business Operations

Our agricultural business encompasses four specialized operations, each upholding traditional Vedic methodology and modern hygiene standards:

🐄

Indigenous Gir Cow Sanctuary

Home to over 150 purebred Bos Indicus Gir cows. We focus on ethical breeding, herbal fodder nutrition, open-paddock exercise, and loving calf-first milking routines.

🏺

Vedic Bilona Ghee Craft

Small-batch production following ancient Charaka Samhita guidelines: A2 milk is cultured in clay pots, churned with wooden bilona ladles, and clarified over firewood.

🪵

Wood Cold-Pressed Oil Mills

Traditional 'Vaagai' wooden mortar chekku extraction operating below 40°C. Extracts pure sesame, groundnut, and coconut oils without chemical solvents or heat damage.

🌱

Regenerative Natural Farming

100% pesticide-free agriculture. We enrich farm soil using indigenous cow-dung Jeevamrutha and organic green manure to grow nutrient-dense crops and herbal cow fodder.
