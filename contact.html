DIRECTIONS & ACCESS

How to Reach Brundavan Natural Farms

Located in the tranquil rural outskirts of Warangal, Telangana, our sanctuary is easily accessible by road and rail.

🚗

From Hyderabad by Road

Take NH 163 (Hyderabad – Warangal Highway). It is a smooth, scenic ~2.5 to 3 hour drive. After reaching Warangal bypass, take the Kakatiya Heritage Canal road towards Gaushala Marg.

🚆

From Kazipet / Warangal Railway Junction

Kazipet Junction and Warangal Railway Station are just 12 to 15 kilometers away. Auto-rickshaws and cabs are readily available to drop you directly at our farm gates.

📍

Key Landmark & Signboards

Look for the large stone archway marking "Brundavan Gaushala Marg", located 2 km past the historic Kakatiya Canal bridge. Ample shaded parking is available inside.

COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Farm Visit & Order FAQs

Everything you need to know before visiting our farm or ordering fresh products.

❓ Is advance booking mandatory to visit the farm?

While walk-ins are always welcomed during open hours (6:00 AM – 6:30 PM), informing us a day in advance via WhatsApp or phone helps our staff ensure fresh milk samples and personal attention for your family.

❓ Is there any entry fee for visitors or children?

No. Brundavan Natural Farms is a sanctuary devoted to cow welfare and traditional agriculture. Visiting is completely free of cost for everyone.

❓ Can we buy milk, ghee, and oils directly at the farm?

Yes! Our Farm-Gate Store stocks freshly churned Vedic Bilona Ghee, freshly cold-pressed cooking oils, raw honey, and dry fruits. Fresh A2 milk can be picked up in glass bottles during milking hours.

❓ Which cities do you deliver your products to?

Fresh A2 raw Gir cow milk is delivered daily in sanitized glass bottles across Warangal and select Hyderabad hubs. Vedic Bilona Ghee, Wood Cold-Pressed Oils, and Dry Fruits are delivered nationwide across India with free shipping above ₹999.
