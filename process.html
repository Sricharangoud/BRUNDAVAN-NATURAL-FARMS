The Sacred 6-Step Vedic Bilona Process

Commercial industrial ghee is made by spinning raw cream at high speeds. Brundavan Vedic Ghee is crafted the authentic, Shastra-prescribed 6-step way — starting from pure whole milk turned into cultured curd, hand-churned with wooden bilona, and slow-simmered into golden elixir.

STEP 01 🥛 A2 Cow Milk STEP 02 🏺 Curd Preparation STEP 03 🪵 Bilona Churning STEP 04 🧈 Fresh Butter STEP 05 🔥 Ghee Preparation STEP 06 ✨ Filtering & Packaging
STEP 01
Ahimsa Dawn Milking

Step 1: A2 Cow Milk

At the break of dawn, our calves drink first from the mother cow's teats until completely satisfied. Only the surplus pure A2 milk is gently hand-milked into sterilized brass containers. Collected from free-grazing indigenous Gir cows, this milk is naturally abundant in A2 beta-casein protein and enriched with revitalizing solar energy (*Surya Ketu Nadi*).

• Calves Nurtured First • 100% Gir Cow A2 Milk • Zero Hormones or Oxytocin
STEP 02
Clay Pot Culturing & Fermentation

Step 2: Curd Preparation

The pure A2 milk is gently simmered in porous earthenware clay pots (*Handis*) over low-heat biofuel embers, preserving heat-sensitive vitamins without scorching. Once cooled to room temperature, the milk is inoculated with our heirloom farm starter culture. Over 12 to 14 hours of natural fermentation, whole milk transforms into thick, creamy, prebiotic-dense curd rich in active probiotic cultures.

• Porous Terracotta Handis • Heirloom Starter Culture • 12-Hour Whole Curd Setting
STEP 03
Bi-Directional Churning at Brahma Muhurta

Step 3: Bilona Churning

Before sunrise in the tranquil Brahma Muhurta hours, the whole cultured curd is poured into large earthen vessels. A handcrafted wooden staff (*Bilona*) made of neem or vaagai wood is spun rhythmically in clockwise and counter-clockwise directions using a cotton rope. This gentle bi-directional vortex churns the curd cold without generating heat friction, protecting delicate milk enzymes.

• Pure Vaagai/Neem Wood Staff • Two-Way Rope Churning • Zero Heat Friction
STEP 04
Hand-Skimmed Makkhan Harvest

Step 4: Fresh Butter

As the wooden bilona completes its rhythmic cycle, glorious mounds of soft, creamy butter (*Makkhan*) float naturally to the surface. Our skilled artisans delicately gather and hand-skim this fresh golden butter, completely separating it from the cooling, gut-soothing digestive buttermilk (*Chaas*). The butter is gently rinsed in pure mountain water to eliminate all remaining buttermilk traces.

• Pure Hand-Harvested Butter • Clean Buttermilk Separation • Living Enzyme Rich
STEP 05
Traditional Brass Pot Clarification

Step 5: Ghee Preparation

The pure hand-harvested butter is transferred into heavy, traditional tinned brass vessels (*Kadai*) and placed over a continuous gentle, slow flame. Water moisture gradually evaporates as the milk solids lightly brown and caramelize at the bottom, producing an intoxicating nutty, sacred aroma. In the final moments of clarification, fresh sacred betel leaves are dipped to infuse natural antioxidants and longevity.

• Heavy Brass Boilers • Slow Fire Clarification • Sacred Nutty Aroma & Betel Leaf
STEP 06
Muslin Filtration & Glass Packing

Step 6: Filtering and Packaging

The warm golden nectar is carefully strained through multiple layers of unbleached organic muslin cloth to eliminate every trace of fine toasted sediment. The pure, clear liquid gold is then poured warm into sterilized, food-grade glass jars. As it cools naturally without artificial refrigeration, it crystallizes into the prized, aromatic granular (*Danedaar*) texture cherished across India.

• Pure Muslin Filtering • Food-Grade Glass Jars • Granular Danedaar Texture
