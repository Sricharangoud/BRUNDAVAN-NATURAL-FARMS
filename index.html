The Sacred Vedic Bilona Ghee Journey

Why is authentic Bilona Ghee prized over commercial melted butter? At Brundavan Natural Farms, every drop goes through the sacred 6-stage transformation.

01

A2 Cow Milk

Collected hygienically from free-grazing native Gir cows at farm sunrise in sterile brass vessels.

02

Curd Preparation

Boiled over clay stoves, cooled naturally, and cultured overnight in porous earthenware pots.

03

Bilona Churning

Traditional two-way wooden bilona gently churns set curd before dawn to extract pure butterfat without friction heat.

04

Fresh Butter

Wholesome makkhan floats to the top, collected with care and washed in sweet cold spring water.

05

Ghee Preparation

Slowly simmered over firewood embers in thick brass vessels until it turns crystal golden with divine nutty aroma.

06

Filtering & Packing

Filtered through fine double muslin cloth and poured into airtight, sanitized glass jars.
